**Tatmeen** (تطمين) is online mental-health care for Saudi Arabia: text, voice and video sessions with doctors and specialists licensed by the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties. Licensed by the Saudi Ministry of Health, licence no. 1400050350. Live since 2024, in Arabic and English.

- **Tatmeen**, the app for booking sessions. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6473836983) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.TatmeenApp.user)
- **Tatmeen Provider**, the app for the doctors and specialists who hold them. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6473837088) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.TatmeenApp.consultant)
- **[tatmeen.sa](https://tatmeen.sa/en/)**, mental-health articles and a directory of care providers.
